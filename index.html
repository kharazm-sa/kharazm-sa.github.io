About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Islamic Azad University, Tehran North Branch, currently focused on front-end development. My work so far lives at the intersection of visual design and how systems actually work underneath.

Most of my front-end practice has gone into building real, functional layouts — a restaurant menu, an auth flow, a small business site — using semantic HTML, CSS and Tailwind. In parallel, I've worked through networking coursework based on the CompTIA Network+ curriculum, and taught myself Git, version control workflows, and command-line tooling from scratch.

I like projects that reward patience: reading the error message instead of guessing, understanding why a layout breaks instead of nudging it until it looks fine.
